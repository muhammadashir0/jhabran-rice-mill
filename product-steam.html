This pack, not the others

Green sack. Steam 1121.

You are buying Ziyarat Kainat Steam 1121. Extra-long grain, steamed at the mill. When the lid lifts, the grains should stand long and separate.

If the pot must wait — catering, a degh, a steam table — take Sella 1121 instead. That kernel is parboiled and holds.

Steam or sella?

Steam 1121 for aroma and a soft plate. Sella 1121 when the rice has to sit. Same variety. Different process.

In the kitchen

How to cook this rice.

Rinse until the water runs almost clear. Soak 20–30 minutes. About 1¾ cups water to 1 cup soaked rice. Boil, then cover on low until the steam holes open. Rest five minutes. Fluff with a fork.

If the plate is sticky, too much water or no soak. 1121 steam should not clump.
