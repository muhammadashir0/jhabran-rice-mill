This pack, not the others

What you are buying.

This is the green Ziyarat sack. The grain is 1121 Kainat, extra-long, and it is steamed — not parboiled.

Steam keeps the kernel softer and more aromatic. When the lid lifts, the grains should stand long and separate. Broken rice is not sold as this pack.

Steam or sella?

Choose Steam 1121 when you want aroma and a soft plate. Choose Kainat Sella 1121 (purple, 50 kg) when the pot must wait — catering, degh, a steam table.

In the kitchen

How to cook this rice.

Rinse until the water runs almost clear. Soak 20–30 minutes. Salt the water. Use about 1¾ cups water to 1 cup soaked rice. Bring to a boil, then cover on low until the steam holes open. Rest five minutes. Fluff with a fork.

If the plate is sticky, you used too much water or skipped the soak. 1121 steam should not clump.
