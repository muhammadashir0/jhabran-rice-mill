This pack, not the others

What you are buying.

Classic is Ziyarat’s gold line. This pack is Kainat basmati sella in 25 kg — not the purple 50 kg 1121 sella, and not steam.

Sella (parboiled) grain stays firmer. Use it when the pot is large or the rice must wait. For Classic in steam, see Classic Basmati Steam.

Classic sella or 1121 sella?

This is the 25 kg gold Classic sella. The purple Kainat Sella 1121 is the 50 kg volume sack. Same mill. Different pack, different weight.

In the kitchen

How to cook this rice.

Rinse. Soak 25–30 minutes. Salt. Water a little above the rice. Boil, then low steam. Rest. The Classic sella grain should stay long on the tray.
