01 — Intake

Paddy checked at the mill

Paddy is received at Jandiala Road, Jhabran. Moisture and grain length are checked before milling starts. Weak lots are not packed.

02 — Dry

Dried so the grain keeps

Wet paddy heats and spoils in store. Drying to a safe moisture is done before husking so the lot stays sound until packing and loading.

03 — Steam or sella

Steam or sella

Steam for a softer plate. Sella (parboiled) for a firmer grain that holds in a degh. Fragrance is from the paddy, not added.

04 — Mill and sort

Husk off. Clean grain only.

Husking, polishing, then sorting. Broken, chalky and sooty grains are taken out. What is packed is extra-long, 100% clean rice — the line printed on the sack.

05 — Pack and load

Pack and load

Mill name and phones on the sack. Stored dry.

06 — Load

Out the gate

Truck or container from Jhabran, Sheikhupura. The port is written with the order.
