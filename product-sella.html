This pack, not the others

Purple sack. Sella 1121.

You are buying Ziyarat Kainat Sella 1121. Extra-long, parboiled. The kernel is firmer than steam and less likely to mash if the pot sits.

For a softer home plate take Steam 1121. For the gold Classic sella sack take Classic Basmati Sella.

This sella or Classic?

This is the purple 1121 sella line. Classic is the gold Kainat basmati sella sack. Same mill. Different mark on the bag.

In the kitchen

How to cook this rice.

Rinse well — sella throws more starch at first. Soak 30 minutes. A little more water than steam. Boil, then low steam. The grain should stay long when cooked rice has to wait.

Do not treat this as broken rice. Packed as whole extra-long 1121.

Before you enquire

Product questions.

If a detail is not listed here, write it in the enquiry. Specifications are confirmed for each order.

What does sella mean here?

Sella is parboiled rice. The paddy is parboiled before milling, which helps the cooked grain hold its shape.

Is this the same as Steam 1121?

No. Same 1121 variety family, different process. Steam is softer; sella is firmer in the pot.

Which bag weights are offered?

Discuss 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg and 25 kg with the mill.
