Our heritage · since 1975

From Jhabran to the table.

Founded by Saith Hanif in 1975, Saith Hanif Rice Mills is based on Jandiala Road, Jhabran, Sheikhupura, Punjab, Pakistan. Our business brings together rice milling, sorting and packaging under the Ziyarat, Sultan and Wazir product brands.

From paddy to pack

Every stage follows the selected product and the requirements agreed for the order.

01

Paddy intake

Paddy is received, weighed and checked as part of the intake process.

02

Steam or sella

Processing follows the selected product and agreed requirements.

03

Milling

Husk and bran are removed to produce milled rice.

04

Sorting

Sorting equipment helps separate unwanted material and grain according to product requirements.

05

Packaging

Rice is packed under our brands or an agreed private-label specification.

06

Dispatch

Orders are prepared for transportation from our mill in Jhabran, Pakistan.
