From paddy to pack

Six stages. The sequence follows the order.

We mill and pack in Jhabran. Steam or sella is applied as the product requires. Grain that fails the sort does not go in the pack. No throughput figures on this page.

  1. 01

    Paddy intake

    Received, weighed and recorded. Moisture checked before processing.

  2. 02

    Steam or sella

    Processing follows the selected product, as applicable.

  3. 03

    Milling

    Husk and bran removed to produce milled rice.

  4. 04

    Sorting

    Unwanted material and grain that fails the process are taken out.

  5. 05

    Packaging

    Packed to the agreed brand or private-label mark and bag weight.

  6. 06

    Dispatch

    Prepared for transport from Jhabran. Port written with the order.

Our process

Before you confirm an order

Quality starts with the details.

Agree variety, process, moisture, broken content, sorting and bag weight before a quotation is final. We do not print ISO or HACCP seals on this website.

  • Rice variety and grain length — short grain and B-1 are not whole extra-long 1121.
  • Processing type — steam or sella (parboiled).
  • Moisture, broken percentage and sorting, confirmed for the order.
  • Packaging — 5 / 10 / 20 / 25 kg, subject to confirmation.
  • Samples and inspection at load, if you appoint the agency.
