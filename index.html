From Jhabran to the world

A legacy in rice milling since 1975.

Founded by Saith Hanif in 1975, Saith Hanif Rice Mills brings together rice milling, sorting and packaging at Jandiala Road, Jhabran, Sheikhupura, Punjab, Pakistan.

Our portfolio features three product brands — Ziyarat, Sultan and Wazir — offering different rice varieties and processing styles for diverse market requirements.

We welcome enquiries from importers, distributors, wholesalers and businesses seeking a Pakistani rice supplier.

From paddy to pack

Our milling process

01

Paddy intake

Paddy is received, weighed and checked as part of the intake process.

02

Steam or sella

Processing follows the selected product and agreed requirements.

03

Milling

Husk and bran are removed to produce milled rice.

04

Sorting

Sorting equipment helps separate unwanted material and grain according to product requirements.

05

Packaging

Rice is packed under our brands or an agreed private-label specification.

06

Dispatch

Orders are prepared for transportation from our mill in Jhabran, Pakistan.

Looking to source rice from Pakistan?

For trade enquiries, please include

  • Rice variety and processing type
  • Required bag weight and packaging
  • Quantity in bags, metric tonnes or containers
