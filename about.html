About Saith Hanif Rice Mills

A tradition rooted in Punjab. A business built around rice.

Founded by Saith Hanif in 1975, Saith Hanif Rice Mills is based on Jandiala Road, Jhabran, Sheikhupura, Punjab, Pakistan.

Our business brings together rice milling, sorting and packaging, serving the market through our product brands Ziyarat, Sultan and Wazir.

Our portfolio includes selected 1121 rice varieties, basmati, steam and sella rice, short-grain rice and other rice products. Each product serves a different set of requirements, from packaging preferences to intended market applications.

We continue to build our business around our milling heritage, practical product knowledge and direct communication with buyers. Our aim is to make it easier for customers to understand our products, discuss their requirements and explore potential supply arrangements.

From our home in Punjab, we welcome conversations with wholesalers, distributors, importers and businesses interested in rice products from Pakistan.

Our approach

From paddy to pack.

Rice production involves a sequence of processes that prepare the grain for its intended product and packaging requirements.

01

Paddy intake

Receiving, weighing and recording relevant intake details.

02

Steam or sella

Processing according to the selected product requirements.

03

Milling

Removing husk and bran to produce milled rice.

04

Sorting

Using sorting equipment to separate unwanted material and grain according to the process.

05

Packaging

Packing rice under the selected brand or an agreed private-label specification.

06

Dispatch

Preparing orders for transportation from Jhabran, Pakistan.
