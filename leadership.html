Founder · 1975

Saith Hanif founded the mill in 1975. The work is still paddy in, bags out — on Jandiala Road, Jhabran, Sheikhupura.

The mill started under Saith Hanif. The gate is still Jandiala Road. Bags leave as Ziyarat, Sultan or Wazir. Extra-long 1121 where the pack says so. A photograph and a longer note from the founder will sit here when they are sent.

Vision

Extra-long 1121 packed in Jhabran, cooked on tables in Asia, the Middle East, and Europe. Grain as the sack says. Port as written.

Mission

Take paddy at the gate. Write moisture. Steam or sella as ordered. Mill, sort, pack, load. What fails the sort does not go in the sack. Same mill for Ziyarat, Sultan and Wazir.
