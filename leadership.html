A legacy built through experience

The mill still carries his name.

Saith Hanif established Saith Hanif Rice Mills in 1975 in Jhabran, Sheikhupura, Punjab, Pakistan. The mill still stands on Jandiala Road. The work is still milling, sorting and packing rice.

This page does not invent a personal biography, education, awards or years in a particular office. What is documented here is the mill: a name, a place, a founding year, and three product brands — Ziyarat, Sultan and Wazir.

Leadership, for us, means keeping that record honest: the grain, the pack, the specification, and the conversation with the buyer.
