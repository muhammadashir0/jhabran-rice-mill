Leadership & vision

The mill still carries his name.

Saith Hanif is Chairman and Managing Director of Saith Hanif Rice Mills, established in 1979 at Grain Market Jhabran, Sheikhupura, Punjab, Pakistan. Day-to-day leadership of the mill is with Saith Javaid Hanif, Chief Executive Officer.

The mill stands on Sheikhupura–Hafizabad Road. The work is milling, sorting and packing rice, offered under Ziyarat, Sultan and Wazir — twelve listed products. Specifications, packaging and availability are confirmed with each order.

This page does not invent qualifications, awards or industry posts. Leadership here means keeping that record honest: the grain, the pack, the specification, and the conversation with the buyer.
