How an enquiry works

Write the product, the weight, the quantity and the port.

Choosing a rice supplier is more than comparing pack names. Buyers need variety, processing type, packaging, order quantity and destination before a quotation can be useful.

Please include, as far as you know them:

  • Product and brand — Ziyarat, Sultan or Wazir, or private label.
  • Variety and process — for example Steam 1121, Sella 1121, basmati or short grain.
  • Packaging size — 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg or 25 kg, or another weight to discuss.
  • Quantity — bags, metric tonnes or containers.
  • Destination — country and discharge port.
  • Specifications — moisture, broken percentage, sorting or other requirements.
  • Private-label artwork — where applicable.

You do not need every field complete to send a first message. Missing details can be discussed.

Quotation, availability, inspection and shipment arrangements must be confirmed for each order.

Before you write

Clear questions. Direct answers.

What should the first message include?

Product name, bag weight if known, estimated quantity (bags, tonnes or containers) and destination port. If you are not sure of the variety, say what the rice is for.

Can you pack our brand?

Private-label projects are subject to availability, packaging feasibility, agreed quantities, approved artwork and written confirmation. Use Design a pack, then send the mill the file.

Do you ship to my country?

We welcome enquiries that name a discharge port. Availability, inspection and shipment are confirmed for each order. We do not publish a list of established destination countries on this site.

Who inspects the load?

You appoint the inspection agency. Cook a sample before you finalise price. Say in the enquiry if inspection is required.
