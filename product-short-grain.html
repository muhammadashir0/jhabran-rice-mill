This pack, not the others

Short grain. Yellow sack.

You are buying Ziyarat Short Grain. A shorter, closer kernel. Not extra-long. Not broken B-1.

Sultan also packs short grain — Sultan Short Grain — if the buyer wants that mark.

Short grain or B-1?

Short grain is whole short rice. B-1 is broken. Different packs. Write the one you want.

In the kitchen

How to cook this rice.

Rinse. Short grain wants a little less water than 1121. Soak 15–20 minutes. Lid on low. It cooks closer — that is the point.

Before you enquire

Product questions.

If a detail is not listed here, write it in the enquiry. Specifications are confirmed for each order.

Is this extra-long rice?

No. Short-grain rice has a shorter kernel. Do not order it as a substitute for 1121.

Typical uses?

Dishes that do not need extra-long grain, including khichdi and similar preparations. Confirm the application with the mill if you are unsure.
