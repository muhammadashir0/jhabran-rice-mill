This pack, not the others

Everyday basmati.

You are buying Ziyarat Basmati. Fragrant rice for the family pot. It is not packed as extra-long 1121.

For extra-long 1121 take Steam 1121 or Sella 1121. For a lower rung take Awami.

Basmati or 1121?

1121 is the extra-long Kainat line. This sack is everyday basmati. Do not order this if the buyer asked for 1121.

In the kitchen

How to cook this rice.

Rinse. Soak 20 minutes. Water just above the rice. Boil, then low lid. Rest and fluff.
