01 — Length

Length of the grain

1121 is extra-long. We look at a sample, not only the lot number on the truck. Broken rice is packed as B-1, not as whole grain.

02 — Clean

Sortex. No sooty grain.

The sack prints “100% Sootys Clean”. That is this mill’s line for optical sort: no sooty grain, no stone, no paddy left in the bag. What fails the sort does not go in the pack.

03 — Moisture

Dry enough to keep

Too wet and the stack heats. Too dry and the grain breaks in the mill. Moisture is checked before milling and again before packing, so the sack stays sound in store and on the road.

04 — Cook

Cook a sample

If it comes out sticky, short or dull, that lot is not packed. No flavour is added.

Certificates
