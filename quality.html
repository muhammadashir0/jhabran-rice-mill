What to discuss

Agree the specification before the quotation is final.

Rice is not a single commodity. Variety, grain length, processing type and packing weight all change what leaves the mill.

Saith Hanif Rice Mills in Jhabran, Sheikhupura, welcomes buyers who want those details written clearly. We do not list ISO, HACCP or laboratory certificates on this website because we will not imply seals we have not documented here.

If you need a sample, a third-party inspection at load, or a lab test, say so in the enquiry so it can be arranged for that order.

Specification points

Practical checks buyers should raise.

01

Rice variety and grain length

1121 extra-long, other basmati, short grain and broken rice are different products. Name the pack you want. Do not treat short grain or B-1 as whole extra-long rice.

02

Processing type

Steam and sella (parboiled) cook differently. Steam is generally softer; sella holds better when cooked rice must wait. Confirm the process on the pack.

03

Moisture

Moisture is checked in the mill because grain that is too wet does not keep, and grain that is over-dried can break. Agree the moisture you need for the destination market.

04

Broken-grain percentage

Broken content should be stated for whole-grain packs. B-1 is broken rice packed as broken — it is not a whole-grain specification.

05

Sorting requirements

Optical sorting is used to take out unwanted material and sooty grain. The bag line “Sootys clean” refers to this sort. Tell us if your market needs a tighter sort.

06

Packaging, samples and inspection

Bag weights in this range are 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg and 25 kg, subject to confirmation. Cook a sample before you argue price. Inspection at load is arranged when you appoint the agency.
